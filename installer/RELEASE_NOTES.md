## Install (no terminal)

This build replaces the earlier "Spread" test builds automatically.

1. Download **SuperSpread-…-Installer.pkg** below.
2. Double-click it. If macOS says it "can't verify the developer": click **Done**, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway** next to the Spread installer, then enter your Mac password.
3. Click through the installer (Continue → Install). It puts the AU and VST3 in your Mac's plug-in folders.
4. Open Logic. If SuperSpread doesn't show up: **Logic Pro → Settings → Plug-in Manager → Reset & Rescan Selection**.
5. Insert it on a track: **Audio FX → Audio Units → Mau Audio → SuperSpread**. On a mono track, choose the **Mono → Stereo** version.
